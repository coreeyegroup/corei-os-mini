# COREI-OS-MINI — Owner Decisions after Document Index v1.1

These decisions were made by the project owner after the v1.1 baseline. Where they conflict with
the baseline documents, these decisions govern. Blueprint decisions DEC-01 … DEC-17 remain in force
except where amended below.

| ID | Date | Decision | Supersedes / amends |
|---|---|---|---|
| DEC-18 | 10 Nov 2026 | **COREI-OS-MINI is a production trading platform, not a paper or demo prototype.** Live accounts may be registered and used. | Amends DEC-09 and the "paper-only" statements in Index v1.1, Master Work Prompt v1.1, Technology Inventory v1.3 and Process Flow v1.4 §5.7. |
| DEC-19 | 10 Nov 2026 | **Live order sending is governed by a firm-wide switch per environment (DEMO, LIVE), off by default.** Turning it on requires a recorded reason and typed confirmation of the environment name; every change is audited. An account sends orders only if its connection and provider are active, its permission is TRADING, its credential allows trading, a provider adapter exists, and the switch for its environment is on. | Implements Blueprint §13.3 kill controls at firm scope and principle 13 (explicit authorisation before live activity). |
| DEC-20 | 10 Nov 2026 | **The earlier configuration registries are removed and replaced** by one Configuration domain: providers, connections, accounts, instruments, symbol mappings, credentials, execution controls and an append-only audit log (migration 009). Existing configuration records were deleted, not migrated. | Replaces migrations 003–008 behaviour and the separate broker-account, connector-profile and terminal-installation screens. |
| DEC-21 | 10 Nov 2026 | **Launch platforms: MT5 and broker REST/WebSocket APIs.** MT5 logins are stored per account; REST API keys or OAuth clients are stored per connection. Other platforms are added as new adapter types. | Narrows Technology Inventory v1.3 §2 "Provider connection". |
| DEC-22 | 10 Nov 2026 | **Environment belongs to the connection** and is fixed after creation; every account inherits it. A connection's platform, provider and code are also fixed; changing them means creating a new connection. | Implements Blueprint §8.2 "an account cannot be silently rebound". |
| DEC-23 | 10 Nov 2026 | **Four roles, held in the database:** Administrator (users only), Trading operations (configuration and credentials; may turn order sending off), Risk approver (turns order sending on; approves strategy versions; activates deployments), Observer (read only). A user may hold several. Administrators cannot change their own roles, and at least one active administrator must remain. The existing server login becomes the first administrator on its first sign-in. | Implements Process Flow v1.4 §7 and Blueprint §15 role separation. |
| DEC-24 | 10 Nov 2026 | **Four-eyes on LIVE activation:** a deployment on a LIVE account must be activated by a different person from the one who proposed it. Strategy-version approval needs the Risk approver role but not a second person, so a single approver can work on DEMO. | Blueprint §15 "must not approve own … live activation change". |
| DEC-25 | 10 Nov 2026 | **Deployment matrix:** a strategy trades only through an explicit, ACTIVE deployment row of strategy version × instrument × account, which names its market-data source (any connection mapping that instrument for data). Approved strategy versions are immutable, enforced in the database. Any edit returns a deployment to PROPOSED. | Implements Blueprint §8.5 and DEC-12/DEC-13. |
| DEC-26 | 11 Nov 2026 | **An MT5 server and its addresses are stored once, on the connection; accounts point to it.** One connection per provider × environment × server. Adding an MT5 account is one step (login, password, server): the server's connection is reused when it exists — server names compared ignoring case and spacing — or created in the same transaction. Addresses the account brings that the connection lacks are added to it unticked, so other accounts are unaffected. | Implements Blueprint §8.2 (explicit account ↔ connection binding) while keeping the MT5 login → server order of work. |
| DEC-27 | 11 Nov 2026 | **Each MT5 account chooses how it uses its server's addresses**, switchable at any time without losing data: SERVER (the connection's ticked addresses in its order — default), FASTEST (the same, fastest by the latest latency test first) or ACCOUNT (its own ordered list, which must come from its server's addresses). An account's own list is kept when the mode is switched. If the broker drops addresses an account chose, they are skipped and flagged; if none remain the account falls back to the server's addresses and is flagged. One rule (`mt5_routing.py`) serves readiness, screens and the adapter. | New; keeps routing options open (more modes can be added to the same field). |
| DEC-28 | 11 Nov 2026 | **Latency is measured from the COREI server**, by TCP connect only (no MT5 handshake, no login, nothing sent): best and median of three attempts per address. Only public internet addresses are tested; private, loopback and reserved addresses are refused. Results are kept as append-only history per connection so routing can later use trends and, once the adapter exists, real order round-trip and slippage per account × address. | New. |
| DEC-29 | 11 Nov 2026 | **No option is narrowed at this stage.** Both MT5 connection methods stay available per connection (`adapter`: MTAPI_DIRECT — licensed SDK, no terminal — or TERMINAL_BRIDGE); own, firm and client accounts are all supported; e-mail, Telegram and webhook alerts are all supported. The choice is made per record and can be changed later. | Keeps owner decision 1 open without blocking other work. |
| DEC-30 | 11 Nov 2026 | **Facts the broker decides are read from the broker, not assumed.** Margin mode (hedging/netting), account group, leverage, stop-out, order/position caps and each symbol's rules (lot min/step/max, filling modes, stops level, trade mode) are stored with the time read and their source (BROKER or MANUAL). Trading is blocked while margin mode or the broker server clock is unknown. Every order is checked against the account's latest symbol rules before sending. | Implements Blueprint §8 account binding and prevents demo-works/live-rejects failures. |
| DEC-31 | 11 Nov 2026 | **Four-eyes on LIVE changes**: adding addresses to a LIVE connection, creating or reactivating one, replacing a LIVE credential and LIVE one-step account set-up become change requests that a different person must approve. Secrets in a request are encrypted and erased once decided. The policy is on by default; a Risk approver may switch it off with a recorded reason (alerted, and shown as a warning on every screen). | Extends DEC-24 to the address and credential paths a password could leak through. |
| DEC-32 | 11 Nov 2026 | **Step-up re-authentication** (password re-entered within the last 5 minutes, policy `reauth_minutes`) for saving or removing credentials, turning order sending ON, deciding approvals, changing policies and user administration. Turning order sending OFF never needs it. | New. |
| DEC-33 | 11 Nov 2026 | **Secrets:** each MT5 account may hold a master and an investor password; read-only jobs use the investor password. Every read of a stored secret is logged (append-only `cfg_credential_use`). Each ciphertext records the key id; `scripts/rotate-credential-key.py` re-encrypts everything in one transaction. Moving the key off the database host (key service or hardware key) remains recommended. | Extends the vault (0.8.0). |
| DEC-34 | 11 Nov 2026 | **Archive, do not delete,** providers, connections, accounts and account groups once they have history (deployments, credential use, latency or login records). Archived records are read-only, hidden from lists by default and restorable. | New. |
| DEC-35 | 11 Nov 2026 | **Client accounts** (managed for someone else) need a mandate reference before they may trade, and mandate expiry is tracked. Whether a licence is needed depends on the jurisdiction; legal advice is required before operating client accounts. | New; not legal advice. |
| DEC-36 | 11 Nov 2026 | **Indian broker APIs (SEBI retail algo framework, from 1 Apr 2026):** the account holder completes the broker's 2FA every trading day (OAuth redirect or TOTP + PIN, neither stored); tokens expire 03:30 IST; the token's client ID must equal the account's; the API app's registered static IP must equal this server's outbound IP; orders are rate-limited per connection (10/s). | New. |
| DEC-37 | 11 Nov 2026 | **Alerts** are queued in the same transaction as the change that causes them (durable outbox, retried 5 times) for LIVE changes, approvals needed, order-sending switches, connection/login failures, address fallbacks and expiries. | New. |
| DEC-38 | 11 Nov 2026 | **Order size is never fixed in configuration.** Each order's size is decided per order from the strategy, the signal's strength and the portfolio's risk (sizing policy: G-P3-06). Configuration holds only limits that are not ours to choose: the contract's smallest increment, and the broker's per-account, per-symbol minimum, step and maximum read from the broker. The pre-send check rounds the chosen size to the broker's step and refuses sizes the broker would reject. | Owner direction 11 Nov 2026: "lot … could not be fixed or narrow". |
| DEC-39 | 11 Nov 2026 | **Real data is read from MT5 through one read-only login** (investor password preferred, master only if none): account facts, server clock offset and every symbol's broker limits are stored with the read, the address that answered and every address tried. The password reaches the reader on stdin only; the reader never calls order methods. SDK fields are matched by name and the raw facts are always kept, so a symbol that cannot be normalised is listed, not lost. | Fills G-C-07 for MT5; the streaming adapter (G-P1-01) reuses the same login path. |
| DEC-40 | 11 Nov 2026 | **Infrastructure console with a spreadsheet-style PostgreSQL editor.** Administrators can view, filter, sort, edit, add and delete rows; every change needs a reason and a re-entered password, is checked against the value shown (refused if changed meanwhile) and logged before/after in the append-only `infra_data_edits` and the audit log. Secret columns are never returned; logs, secrets, sessions and migration history are always read-only; configuration tables with their own four-eyes and readiness screens are read-only unless the policy `infra_edit_controlled_tables` is turned on. The SQL console is one statement in a READ ONLY transaction with a 5 s limit. Services are shown, not restarted (the web service has no root rights). | Owner request 11 Nov 2026. |
| DEC-41 | 11 Nov 2026 | **Market data is stored in open-source layers only:** a daily journal of every message as received (gzip, fsync'd) → live prices in memory → recent ticks and bars in PostgreSQL (ticks split by day) → nightly Parquet history files read with DuckDB. The database never holds a price the journal lacks (the journal is written first in every batch). TimescaleDB is not used: its compression and continuous aggregates are not under an open-source licence. | Owner choice 11 Nov 2026: "open source and simplicity". |
| DEC-42 | 11 Nov 2026 | **Collection plans decide what is collected** (account × broker symbol × data types × bar timeframes × schedule × retention × quality rules) and are the only way collection starts. Every price carries the broker's time (converted to UTC with the server offset read at login), COREI's receive time and a sequence number. Bars are built by the collector from bid prices; broker history fills gaps only and never overwrites bars built from live prices. Starting a LIVE trading feed is four-eyes (policy `md_four_eyes_trading_feeds`). | |
| DEC-43 | 11 Nov 2026 | **Feed quality is measured continuously and acted on:** latency, silence, frozen prices, spread, spikes, update count and deviation from other sources of the same instrument, scored per minute. A primary trading feed with "switch" set moves to its backup after 30 s unhealthy (backup healthy) and back after 5 minutes healthy; every switch is an event and an alert. Every order check also checks the live price (age, spread, feed state). | |
| DEC-44 | 11 Nov 2026 | **Data integrity:** history files are written under a temporary name, row-counted with DuckDB, fingerprinted (SHA-256) and renamed before the rows leave PostgreSQL; every file is re-hashed nightly (changed or missing = CRITICAL alert); backups copy the database (pg_dump) and the files to COREI_BACKUP_DIR, never overwriting a good backup copy with a changed file, and warn when the backup is on the same disk. Disk use pauses research and monitoring plans before any trading feed is touched. | Owner requirement: "unseen core" and no silent data loss. |

## Still required before the first live order

DEC-18 removes the paper-only restriction; it does not by itself make live trading ready. The
blueprint's acceptance criteria (§18) still apply. Before the LIVE switch is turned on, the
following must exist and be verified: a provider adapter, the order lifecycle with UNKNOWN-outcome
handling, signals from live data, risk limits with reservations, and reconciliation (see
docs/GAP_REGISTER.md). Roles and four-eyes activation are in place (DEC-23, DEC-24).
